Vocabulary-level reference
Cambridge English A2 Key / A2 Key for Schools Vocabulary List, August 2025:
https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/Images/506886-a2-key-2020-vocabulary-list.pdf
Cambridge English B1 Preliminary / Preliminary for Schools Vocabulary List, August 2025:
https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/Images/506887-b1-preliminary-vocabulary-list.pdf
The original PDFs, test questions, Cambridge examples, and Cambridge logos are not
included in this application. Headwords are normalised and arranged into this
application's own learning topics. This application is not endorsed by Cambridge.
The reference lists are not exhaustive lists of all vocabulary used in the exams.
The mapping from parsed reference headwords is in reference-coverage.tsv.

Bilingual sentence examples
Some sentences and translations are from the Tatoeba Project:
https://tatoeba.org
via the ManyThings English-Mandarin sentence-pair collection:
https://www.manythings.org/anki/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
Per-sentence IDs and author names are retained in sentence-attribution.tsv.
English sentences are unchanged. Chinese translations have been converted from
traditional to simplified Chinese with OpenCC. The source download mirror and
SHA-256 hashes are recorded in coverage.json. The original source's full corpus
is not included. No Tatoeba audio recordings are used.

Offline pronunciation
The included pronunciation files were synthesized locally from the vocabulary
headwords using Microsoft Zira Desktop (en-US), then encoded as MP3. They contain
synthetic speech, not human studio recordings. The voice engine and voice-model
files are not redistributed. The app does not need a network connection or an
installed Android English TTS voice to play them.

Review status
All records, source references, and audio files receive automated integrity
checks. Learner meanings have received a fast editorial scan and targeted
corrections. Corpus sentence/sense alignment and pronunciation have not received
an exhaustive human review. Marked Meaning: word cards are recognition/listening
material and must not be used as sentence or contextual-comprehension exercises.
